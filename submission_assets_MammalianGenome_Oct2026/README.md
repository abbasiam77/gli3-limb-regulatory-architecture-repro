## Scientific scope

The study analyzes 13 curated, functionally supported Gli3 limb
enhancers and compares their molecular signals with those of 41
VISTA Gli3-region elements with reported non-limb activity and no
limb annotation in the source records.

The 41 elements form an annotation-based comparison set, not
experimentally established universal limb-negative controls.

Chromatin, transcription-factor occupancy and chromosome-contact
signals provide contextual and correlative information. The
rank-percentile and empirical AUC analyses describe heterogeneity
and overlap and do not constitute validated enhancer predictors
or establish direct regulatory mechanisms.
