## Scientific interpretation

The analysis distinguishes 13 curated, functionally supported
Gli3 limb enhancers from 41 annotation-based VISTA comparison
elements in the broader Gli3 region.

The comparison elements are not treated as experimentally
validated universal limb-negative controls.

Chromatin, transcription-factor occupancy, chromosome-contact
and AUC analyses are interpreted descriptively and
correlatively, not as proof of predictive performance or
direct regulatory mechanism.
