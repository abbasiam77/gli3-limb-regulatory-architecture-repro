# Main Figure 4: integrated enhancer-signal heatmap

This module supports Main Figure 4 of the September 2026 manuscript revision.

Main Figure 4 presents an integrated rank-percentile view of chromatin and
transcription-factor signal across 54 validated enhancers included in the
locus-matched comparison: 13 curated functionally supported Gli3 limb enhancers
and 41 validated non-limb Gli3-region enhancers.

Signals are interpreted as correlative genomic context rather than direct
evidence of enhancer function, enhancer-promoter interaction, or regulatory
mechanism. Functional validation remains the anchor for enhancer identity.
